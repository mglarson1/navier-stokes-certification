# Licensing status during preparation

No project-wide distribution licence was present in the source material inspected
for this preparation. This draft does not grant a new licence on the authors'
behalf. Final licensing remains to be selected before public release.

The proposed choice is MIT for the authors' original software and CC BY 4.0 for
their original data, figures and documentation. Those choices are proposals,
not effective licence grants. Any third-party material retains its own terms;
a blanket licence must not replace third-party notices.

NumPy, mpmath and Matplotlib are dependencies installed separately, not vendored
in this repository. The frozen evidence archives retain their original content.
Any confirmed licensing explanation for that content will be supplied alongside
the archives rather than changing their bytes.
