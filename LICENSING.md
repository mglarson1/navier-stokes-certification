# Licensing during setup

No project-wide licence is granted by this draft repository. Licence selection
for the authors' original code, data and documentation remains pending before
public release. Third-party material retains its own terms.

The current proposal is MIT for original software and CC BY 4.0 for original
data, figures and documentation. These are proposals, not effective grants.
Each later paper must document the applicable authorship and rights rather than
silently inheriting a blanket licence from Paper 1.

See also the preserved [Paper 1 licensing note](papers/paper1-certified-drag/LICENSING.md).
